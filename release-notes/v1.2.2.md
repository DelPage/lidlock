# LidLock 1.2.2

LidLock now explains any extra approval before it continues and directs you to
the right System Settings page when another step is needed.

## Download

Apple signs the attached `LidLock.dmg` with Developer ID and notarizes it.
Gatekeeper validates both the app and disk image.


## What Changed

- LidLock explains the effect of Stay Awake with Lid Closed before macOS asks
  for administrator approval.
- Restoring Normal Sleep also explains administrator approval before the
  password prompt appears.
- The optional lid approval and Open at login prompts can open Login Items when
  approval must be completed in System Settings.
- Settings shows when approval is still pending.
- Quit guidance keeps LidLock open when normal sleep was not restored, unless
  you explicitly choose to quit and leave the lid setting on.
- The first launch guide and Settings now list which controls need approval.

## Approvals

Most controls work immediately. Stay Awake, Keep Screen On, Walk Away, the menu
bar, and power details do not need approval unless they first need to end Stay
Awake with Lid Closed.

Starting or ending Stay Awake with Lid Closed can require administrator
approval. LidLock explains the request before macOS asks for a password.

If you enable **Allow lid changes without a password** or **Open at login**,
macOS may require approval under **System Settings**, **General**, **Login
Items**. LidLock can open Login Items and shows when approval is pending.

## Replacing Older Copies

Before installing this version, choose **Normal Sleep**, turn off **Allow lid
changes without a password** and **Open at login**, quit LidLock, and remove
older copies. Install the new copy in the main Applications folder.

The [Getting Started guide](https://github.com/DelPage/lidlock/blob/main/GETTING_STARTED.md#replace-older-copies)
has the complete cleanup steps.

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
- A MacBook for Stay Awake with Lid Closed. Other Macs can still use the awake
  and display controls.

## Privacy

LidLock has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, or advertising. It does not
contact any server while you use it. The optional helper stays on your Mac and
changes only the lid sleep setting.

Privacy policy: https://github.com/DelPage/lidlock/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
