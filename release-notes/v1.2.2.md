another step is needed.

## What Changed

- Added clear explanations before macOS asks for an administrator password.
- Added shortcuts to Login Items when macOS needs approval.
- Settings now shows pending approvals.
- If normal sleep cannot be restored, LidLock stays open and explains the
  available choices before quitting.
- Updated first launch and Settings guidance to show which controls need
  approval.

## Approvals

Most features need no approval. Starting or stopping **Stay Awake with Lid
Closed** may require an administrator password. Enabling **Allow lid changes
without a password** or **Open at login** may require approval in **System
Settings**, **General**, **Login Items**. LidLock can open that page for you.

## Replacing Older Copies

Before installing, choose **Normal Sleep**, turn off **Allow lid changes without
a password** and **Open at login**, quit LidLock, and remove older copies.

See the [replacement steps](https://github.com/DelPage/lidlock/blob/main/GETTING_STARTED.md#replace-older-copies).

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
- A MacBook for modes that use the lid. Other Macs can still use the awake and
  display controls.

## Privacy

LidLock has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, or automatic
network access while it runs.
