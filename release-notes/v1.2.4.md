LidLock 1.2.4 fixes the existing Stay Awake with Lid Closed mode.

- Keeps the session unlocked while work continues, without changing your Mac’s lock settings.
- Turns off the screen when the lid closes and restores your brightness when it opens.
- Restores brightness when leaving the mode or quitting, and recovers it on the next launch after an unexpected exit.
- Replaces the instructions to change Lock Screen settings with guidance for the corrected behavior.
