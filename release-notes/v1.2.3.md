LidLock 1.2.3 makes the lid and display choices explicit.

- Keep Screen On and Stay Awake now say “Lid Open” in the window and menu.
- The closed-lid choice explains that work keeps running while the MacBook display turns off.
- Display Off Without Locking explains the macOS password setting and opens Lock Screen settings. It is available in the window, menu, and Settings.
- Mode descriptions remain readable instead of being cut off.

Keeping the Mac awake does not prevent locking. To reopen without unlocking,
choose Stay Awake with Lid Closed and set the macOS “Require password after
screen saver begins or display is turned off” preference to Never. This setting
also applies outside LidLock.
