Stay Awake with Lid Closed now leaves Lock Screen behavior to macOS.

## What Changed

- Renamed the mode to **Stay Awake with Lid Closed**.
- The mode no longer forces the Lock Screen. Your macOS Lock Screen settings
  continue to apply.
- Improved reliability when starting and maintaining the mode.

## Stay Awake with Lid Closed

1. Install LidLock in the Applications folder.
2. Open LidLock and choose **Stay Awake with Lid Closed**.
3. Approve the macOS administrator request.
4. Close the lid.

Choose **Normal Sleep** when you want the usual macOS sleep behavior again.

## Replacing Older Copies

Before installing, choose **Normal Sleep**, turn off **Allow lid changes without
a password** and **Open at login**, quit LidLock, and remove older copies.

See the [replacement steps](https://github.com/DelPage/lidlock/blob/main/GETTING_STARTED.md#replace-older-copies).

## Requirements

- macOS 13 Ventura or newer.
- A MacBook for modes that use the lid. Other Macs can still use the awake and
  display controls.

## Privacy

LidLock has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising, or automatic
network access while it runs.
